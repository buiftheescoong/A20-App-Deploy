# Lesson Plan Quality Checklist

Use this checklist to evaluate generated or uploaded Vietnamese lesson plans. The checker should validate template compliance, teaching-model fit, pedagogical quality, factual grounding, and export readiness.

## Checker Modes

### Internal Mode

Use this mode for lesson plans generated by the system.

Available metadata:

- subject
- grade
- lesson title
- duration
- teaching model
- database lesson objectives/yêu cầu cần đạt
- uploaded or retrieved reference materials

Apply all checks in this document.

### External / Upload Mode

Use this mode for lesson plans uploaded from outside the system.

Available metadata may be incomplete. Apply all checks that can be evaluated from the document itself. Skip metadata-dependent checks only when required metadata is unavailable.

Examples of checks that may be skipped:

- database objective alignment when database objectives are unavailable
- teaching model compliance when the model is neither provided as metadata nor inferable from the lesson plan
- reference material grounding when no reference materials are provided

Skipped checks must be reported in `skipped_checks`, not in `passed_checks`.

## Result Levels

Decision rules take priority over score.

- `Failed`: any `Critical` issue exists, or score < 60.
- `Needs revision`: no critical issues, but at least one `Major` issue exists, or score is 60-84.
- `Passed`: no critical issues, no major issues, and score >= 85. Minor issues may still be listed when they do not block use.

## Severity Levels

- `Critical`: makes the lesson plan unusable or unsafe, or violates a required structure.
- `Major`: important quality issue that should be fixed before use.
- `Minor`: clarity, formatting, completeness, or polish issue that does not block use.

## Scoring

Start from 100 points.

- Critical issue: subtract 25 points each.
- Major issue: subtract 10 points each.
- Minor issue: subtract 3 points each.
- Minimum score is 0.

## Required Output Format

Return the review result in this structure:

```json
{
  "status": "Passed | Needs revision | Failed",
  "score": 100,
  "summary": "Short Vietnamese summary of the overall quality.",
  "issues": [
    {
      "severity": "Critical | Major | Minor",
      "section": "Section or activity name",
      "problem": "What is wrong",
      "suggestion": "Concrete fix"
    }
  ],
  "passed_checks": [
    "Checks that were evaluated and passed"
  ],
  "skipped_checks": [
    {
      "check": "Check name",
      "reason": "Why it could not be evaluated"
    }
  ]
}
```

## 1. Structural Compliance Checks

### 1.1 Lesson Opening

- The lesson plan starts directly with the lesson title.
- The lesson duration appears immediately after or near the title.
- The lesson plan does not include administrative/template labels.
- The title is not empty and matches the requested lesson/chapter/topic when metadata is available.

Severity:

- Missing title: `Critical`
- Title conflicts with metadata: `Critical`
- Missing duration: `Major`
- Administrative/template label included: `Major`

### 1.2 Required Main Sections

The lesson plan must include these sections in order:

1. `I. Mục tiêu / Lesson objectives`
2. `II. Phương pháp dạy học, kĩ thuật dạy học / Teaching methods, teaching techniques`
3. `III. Thiết bị dạy học và học liệu / Learning equipment and materials`
4. `IV. Tiến trình dạy học / Process of organizing teaching activities`

Severity:

- Missing section I or IV: `Critical`
- Missing section II or III: `Major`
- Sections are severely out of order: `Major`
- Minor wording mismatch but meaning is preserved: `Minor`

### 1.3 No Placeholders

The final lesson plan must not contain unresolved placeholders:

- `{{...}}`
- ellipsis-only content
- empty bullets
- empty required table cells

Severity:

- Placeholder in required content: `Critical`
- Placeholder in optional content: `Major`
- Small formatting residue that does not affect meaning: `Minor`

## 2. Objective and Competency Checks

### 2.1 Database Objective Alignment

Internal Mode:

- The lesson plan uses database lesson objectives/yêu cầu cần đạt as the primary source.
- Subject-specific competencies align with the database objectives.
- The lesson plan does not invent objectives that conflict with database objectives.

External / Upload Mode:

- Apply this check only when database objectives or enough metadata are available.
- Otherwise report this check in `skipped_checks`.

Severity:

- Missing database objectives in Internal Mode: `Critical`
- Conflict with database objectives: `Critical`
- Objectives are too generic or not observable: `Major`

### 2.2 Subject-Specific Competency

- The subject competency heading matches the selected subject, for example `Năng lực Sinh học`, `Năng lực Toán học`, `Năng lực Ngữ văn`.
- Competency expressions are concrete and observable.
- Competencies are reflected later in activities.

Severity:

- Wrong subject competency: `Major`
- Competencies not reflected in activities: `Major`
- Minor wording issue: `Minor`

### 2.3 General Competencies

General competencies should be expressed through activities, not only listed by name.

Required general competency areas:

- Năng lực tự học.
- Năng lực giải quyết vấn đề.
- Năng lực giao tiếp và hợp tác.

Severity:

- Missing all general competencies: `Major`
- Listed without activity evidence: `Major`
- One competency is weakly expressed: `Minor`

### 2.4 Qualities / Character

The plan should describe concrete expressions of qualities such as:

- yêu nước
- chăm chỉ
- nhân ái
- trách nhiệm
- trung thực

The checker should not require all five qualities in every lesson, but selected qualities must be realistic and connected to tasks or behaviors.

Severity:

- No qualities section: `Major`
- Qualities are only listed with no behavior or activity link: `Major`
- Quality choice feels weak but acceptable: `Minor`

## 3. Teaching Model Compliance

The checker must identify the selected teaching model from metadata or infer it from the lesson plan.

Supported models:

- `3 giai đoạn`: Khởi động/Xác định vấn đề, Hình thành kiến thức mới, Luyện tập, Vận dụng.
- `5E`: Engage, Explore, Explain, Elaborate, Evaluate.
- `VNEN`: Khởi động, Hình thành kiến thức, Luyện tập, Vận dụng, Tìm tòi/Mở rộng.

Checks:

- Activity names/headings match the selected model's phase structure.
- Activity content matches the purpose of each phase, not only the phase name.
- If the model is unknown in External / Upload Mode, do not fail the plan for model mismatch; report model compliance as skipped unless it is clearly inferable.

Severity:

- Activity structure conflicts with known selected model: `Critical`
- Activity names match model but content is generic or not phase-specific: `Major`
- Model not specified and no metadata available: skip check

## 4. Teaching Methods and Materials Checks

### 4.1 Teaching Methods and Techniques

- Methods and techniques are suitable for the lesson objective, grade, subject, and duration.
- Methods and techniques appear again in the teaching process.
- Group/collaboration methods are present when communication and collaboration competency is claimed.

Severity:

- Methods missing: `Major`
- Methods conflict with activities: `Major`
- Methods are generic but usable: `Minor`

### 4.2 Equipment and Learning Materials

- Equipment and learning materials are specific enough to support the activities.
- Uploaded/reference materials are listed in section III when provided.
- Materials are realistic for classroom use.
- Materials listed in section III are used in at least one activity when applicable.

Severity:

- Missing equipment/materials section: `Major`
- Uploaded materials not listed in section III: `Minor`
- Materials not connected to activities: `Major`
- Materials too vague: `Minor`

## 5. Teaching Process Checks

### 5.1 Required Activities

The teaching process must include all phases required by the selected teaching model.

Default `3 giai đoạn` checks:

1. Hoạt động 1: Khởi động / Xác định vấn đề / Xác định nhiệm vụ học tập.
2. Hoạt động 2: Hình thành kiến thức mới.
3. Hoạt động 3: Luyện tập.
4. Hoạt động 4: Vận dụng.

For `5E` and `VNEN`, use the phase sequence in section 3.

Severity:

- Missing first/new-knowledge core activity for the selected model: `Critical`
- Missing practice, application, extension, or evaluation activity required by the selected model: `Major`
- Activity order is confusing: `Major`

### 5.2 Required Fields in Each Activity

Each activity must include:

- `a) Mục tiêu / Objective`
- `b) Sản phẩm / Products`
- `c) Tổ chức thực hiện / Implementation organization`
- activity duration
- classroom organization format
- assessment method

Severity:

- Missing implementation organization: `Critical`
- Missing objective or product: `Major`
- Missing assessment method in all activities: `Major`
- Missing assessment method in one or two activities: `Minor`
- Missing activity duration in all activities: `Major`
- Some activities missing duration: `Minor`
- Missing classroom organization format in most activities: `Minor`
- Objective/product is vague: `Minor`

### 5.3 Implementation Table Format

Each `Tổ chức thực hiện` section must use a 2-column table:

| Hoạt động của giáo viên / Teacher's activities | Hoạt động của học sinh / Students' activities |
|---|---|
| Chuyển giao nhiệm vụ học tập | Student receives task |
| Thực hiện nhiệm vụ học tập | Student performs task |
| Báo cáo thảo luận | Student reports/discusses |
| Kết luận, nhận định | Student records/finalizes |

Required checks:

- Exactly 2 role columns: teacher activities and student activities.
- All 4 process rows are present.
- Teacher and student cells are not empty.
- Teacher and student actions are logically paired.

Severity:

- Missing implementation table: `Critical`
- Table does not have teacher/student columns: `Critical`
- Missing one or more required process rows: `Major`
- Empty required table cell: `Major`
- Teacher/student actions are not logically paired: `Major`
- Minor table formatting issue: `Minor`

### 5.4 Activity Quality

Each activity should be:

- Appropriate for the grade level.
- Aligned with lesson objectives.
- Clear enough for a teacher to conduct.
- Matched with a concrete student product.
- Feasible within the lesson duration.
- Connected to methods, materials, competencies, and assessment.

Severity:

- Activity unrelated to objectives: `Major`
- Activity impossible or unrealistic for classroom use: `Major`
- Activity is generic but usable: `Minor`

### 5.5 Activity 2 Sub-Activities

If Activity 2 is divided into sub-activities such as `2.1`, `2.2`, each sub-activity must include objective, product, implementation organization, duration, classroom organization, and assessment.

For a single-period 45-minute lesson, Activity 2 should not have more than 3 sub-activities unless the lesson content clearly requires it.

Severity:

- Sub-activity missing required structure: `Major`
- Too many sub-activities for a single-period lesson: `Minor`

### 5.6 Optional Sections Quality

If `Tiểu kết / Sub-conclusion` is included:

- Content must not be empty or placeholder-only.
- Content should summarize key knowledge from the activity.

If `Nội dung ghi vở cho học sinh` is included:

- Content must not be empty or placeholder-only.
- Content should be suitable for student note-taking.

Severity:

- Optional section present but empty/placeholder-only: `Minor`
- Optional section content does not match its activity: `Major`

### 5.7 Assessment Alignment

- At least one explicit assessment method must appear in the lesson plan.
- Each activity should indicate how student outcomes are assessed, for example teacher feedback, product review, peer assessment, self-assessment, observation, rubric, or question response.
- Assessment methods must match objective types:
  - knowledge objective: knowledge check, explanation, question, concept map
  - skill objective: performance task, product, practice result
  - quality/attitude objective: observation, reflection, behavioral evidence

Severity:

- No assessment method anywhere in the plan: `Major`
- Assessment present but not aligned with objectives: `Major`
- Assessment missing in one or two activities but present overall: `Minor`

### 5.8 Duration Consistency

- Total activity time must not exceed the lesson duration.
- Activity durations should be reasonable and balanced.
- Each activity should specify its own duration.

Severity:

- Total duration exceeds lesson duration: `Major`
- No activity has individual duration specified: `Major`
- Some activities missing duration: `Minor`
- Slightly imbalanced timing: `Minor`

## 6. Content Accuracy Checks

### 6.1 Subject Knowledge

- Knowledge must be accurate for the selected subject and grade.
- Formulas, definitions, historical facts, scientific facts, and literary references must not be fabricated.
- If reference materials are provided, subject content must not contradict them.

Severity:

- Factual error in core knowledge: `Critical`
- Unsupported claim that could mislead students: `Major`
- Minor imprecision: `Minor`

### 6.2 Reference Material Grounding

When reference materials are available:

- The plan should use relevant content from the materials.
- The plan should not invent details beyond the provided source when the task requires source-grounded output.
- Important source content should not be omitted if it is central to the lesson.

Severity:

- Contradiction with reference material: `Critical`
- Major unsupported addition: `Major`
- Minor missing detail: `Minor`

## 7. Vietnamese Language and Export Formatting Checks

### 7.1 Language

- Main generated content is in Vietnamese.
- Fixed bilingual headings may include English.
- Vietnamese grammar, tone, and terminology are appropriate for professional lesson plans.

Severity:

- Large section generated in the wrong language: `Major`
- Unprofessional wording: `Major`
- Minor grammar/style issue: `Minor`

### 7.2 Export-Ready Formatting

- Markdown headings are clear and consistent.
- Main sections use Roman numerals in correct order: I, II, III, IV.
- Required bilingual headings are preserved.
- Activity sub-headings include bilingual labels where defined by the template.
- Tables are valid Markdown tables.
- Bullets and numbering are readable.
- No duplicate headings unless intentionally used for sub-activities.

Severity:

- Broken table or unreadable layout: `Major`
- Main section headings missing Roman numerals or severely out of order: `Major`
- Required bilingual label missing from a main section: `Minor`
- Missing bilingual label in an activity sub-heading: `Minor`
- Duplicate/confusing heading: `Minor`
- Small Markdown formatting issue: `Minor`

## 8. AI Guardrail Checks

### 8.1 No Fabricated Sources

- If the lesson plan cites documents, textbooks, or sources, those must be real references that were uploaded, retrieved, or known official materials.
- The plan must not invent citations, author names, publication details, or source titles.

Severity:

- Fabricated citation or source: `Critical`
- Vague citation that cannot be verified: `Major`

### 8.2 Uncertainty Marking

- When specialized subject content cannot be verified from reference materials or known official sources, the plan should mark it as requiring teacher verification.

Severity:

- Unverifiable specialized content presented as certain fact: `Major`
- Minor imprecision without caveat: `Minor`

### 8.3 Database Objective Preservation

- The lesson plan must not alter lesson objectives/yêu cầu cần đạt retrieved from the database without a documented reason.
- Rewriting objectives for clarity is acceptable when the meaning and scope are preserved.

Severity:

- Objective meaning or scope changed from database source: `Critical`
- Objective reworded but meaning preserved: no issue

## 9. Internal Consistency Checks

### 9.1 Method-Activity Consistency

- Teaching methods and techniques listed in section II must be reflected in section IV activities.
- If a method appears in section IV but not in section II, flag it as an inconsistency.

Severity:

- Methods in section II not used in any activity: `Major`
- Methods used in activities not listed in section II: `Minor`

### 9.2 Competency-Activity Consistency

- If `Năng lực giao tiếp và hợp tác` is claimed in section I, at least one activity in section IV must involve pair/group work.
- Competency expressions in section I must be traceable to specific activities.

Severity:

- Communication/collaboration competency claimed but no pair/group activity exists: `Major`
- Competency listed with no matching activity evidence: `Major`
- Weak but traceable connection: `Minor`

### 9.3 Equipment-Activity Consistency

- Equipment and materials listed in section III should be used in at least one activity.
- If reference materials are listed, they should be referenced in relevant activity content.

Severity:

- Equipment/materials listed but never used in any activity: `Minor`
- Reference materials listed but not reflected in relevant activity content: `Minor`

## 10. Safety and Appropriateness Checks

- Content must be age-appropriate.
- No harmful, discriminatory, or inappropriate examples.
- No private student data should appear in the generated plan.
- Activities should be safe for classroom use.

Severity:

- Unsafe or age-inappropriate content: `Critical`
- Privacy leak or personal student data: `Critical`
- Minor sensitivity concern: `Major`

## 11. Final Decision Rules

The checker should:

- Mark `Failed` when any `Critical` issue exists.
- Mark `Needs revision` when there are no critical issues but one or more major issues exist, or score is 60-84.
- Mark `Passed` only when required structure is complete, content is aligned, and no major issue remains.
- Always provide concrete suggestions, not only problem labels.
- Prefer concise, actionable Vietnamese feedback.
- Put checks that could not be evaluated in `skipped_checks`, not `passed_checks`.
